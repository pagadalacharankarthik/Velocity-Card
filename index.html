✦ The Process

How it Works

From submission to publishing — a seamless, quality-controlled workflow designed for busy print shops.

01
Shop Submits Order

Print shop fills out the order form with couple details, wedding info, venue, and uploads bride/groom/couple photos. All securely stored in Supabase.

02
Admin Reviews & Edits

Platform owner reviews photos, selects best images, adjusts positioning using the visual editor, applies filters, typography, and animation settings.

03
QR Mobile Preview

Admin generates a private QR preview link. Scanned on real mobile devices to verify quality, animations, and responsiveness before any client sees it.

04
Payment & Publish

Once payment is verified, invitation is published to a permanent URL. Shop receives the link and QR code to share via WhatsApp with the couple's guests.

05
Live & Memory Mode

Invitation is live for guests to view, RSVP, and send wishes. After the wedding, it transforms into a permanent memory album with all photos and wishes.
